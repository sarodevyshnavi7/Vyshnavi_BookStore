A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

How do I find a book?

Open the Books page and search by book title, author, category or Book ID. You can also use the category dropdown to filter the list.

Can I add a new book?

Yes. Open Add Book and enter a unique Book ID along with the title, author, category, price and stock. The record is saved to the connected database.

What if a book’s details are incorrect?

Use Update, enter the Book ID, load the current details and save the corrected information.

How can I share a suggestion?

Fill in the contact form above with your name, email, subject and message. The form currently displays a confirmation on the website.
